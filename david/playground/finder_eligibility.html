How this works & disclaimer

This is a static prototype: no server, no AI. Your pill selections narrow the programs against a fixed rule set in your browser. Expanding a program reveals illustrative eligibility criteria you can answer yes / no / not sure; the verdict is a simple check of those answers. The criteria are best-guess summaries of Canada.ca program pages for demonstration only — they are not an official eligibility assessment. An officer decides eligibility under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act.
