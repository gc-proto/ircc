the mark

The logo was the method all along

It was never one thing. Read it three ways and it describes how we worked.

A double diamond

Go wide on the problem, narrow it down, go wide on solutions, narrow again. Discover, define, develop, deliver.

The first diamond is the problem. The second is the solution. Hold that thought — it matters at the end of this page.

what made it work

It was never the methodology

The retro was clear about this. Our greatest strength wasn’t a method or a deliverable. It was the people, the culture, and the freedom to experiment, and the way those three fed each other.

People shared what they knew, challenged ideas without making it personal, learned from each other, and got from an idea to a prototype to evidence fast. And we mostly resisted the expectation that the first answer had to be the right one.

the honest part

The final mile

We were often better at getting to the answer than at getting the answer through the final mile and into people’s hands. That’s the part that still stings a little.
