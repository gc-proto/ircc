Post-graduation work permit (PGWP) For recent college or university graduates who want to work and live in Canada temporarily

Processing times

Check processing times

Fees

PGWP: CAN$255

Restoration of student status: CAN$389.75

This could be for you if you:

  • studied full-time at an eligible designated learning institution (DLI)
  • completed a qualifying study program that was at least 8 months long
  • recently completed your program
  • can communicate in English or French (unless you graduated from a flight school)
