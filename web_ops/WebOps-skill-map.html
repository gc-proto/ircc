    French: a separate path

    Some positions are bilingual and need a specific language profile; others don't need French at all. Check the profile on the job poster or ask your manager. The path goes from simple, free habits to part-time training, which management offers to people who are ready to move up.

      Where you are in French

      Use your most recent second language evaluation results, or your best guess. The target is the position's profile: reading, writing and oral, in that order (for example CBC).

      Review together

      Open the employee's map file, then go through it together. The employee rates first in “My map”. The manager adds their rating here. Where you differ by 2 points or more, talk it through with an example, then set the agreed rating. Agreed ratings are what the tree and the team view use.

      Past reviews in this file

        “Record this review” saves today's agreed ratings as a dated snapshot in the file, so the next review shows what moved. Then save the file.

        Team view

        For managers: open everyone's map files at once (select them all in the team folder) to see the team's shape, its gaps and who carries what. Nothing is uploaded: the files are only read in this browser.

        Or drop map files here.

        The yearly cycle

        1. The employee rates themselvesIn “My map”, on the 1-to-5 scale, then saves the file in their folder in the team space.
        2. The manager rates tooBefore the meeting, the manager opens the file, adds their ratings in “Review together”, and saves.
        3. Meet and agreeGo through the gaps of 2 points or more, with examples. Set the agreed ratings, then “Record this review” and save.
        4. Plan training pointsPick the next skills and the hours for the quarter, including set-aside hours for free resources.
        5. See the team pictureThe manager opens all the files in “Team view” to spot gaps and single points of failure.
        6. Check inA short check-in each quarter on the plan; a full review once or twice a year.

        The scale

        Adapted from the Nielsen Norman Group skill mapping method. Rate what you do now, on typical work, not what you could do on a good day.

        Where the files live

        • Each person's map is one small file, like skillmap-alex-tremblay.json. Keep it in a folder only the employee and their manager can open, on approved GC systems (Teams or SharePoint).
        • “Save” updates the same file when your browser allows it (Edge and Chrome). Otherwise it downloads a new copy: replace the old file in the folder with it.
        • This page reads and writes files only on your computer. It doesn't send ratings anywhere.
        • Open this tool from the HTML file itself. In a preview (for example, inside Teams or claude.ai), saving may not work.
        Treat the files as personal information. Skill ratings are about a person. Store them the way your team stores other employee information, and check with your privacy or information management contact if you're unsure.
